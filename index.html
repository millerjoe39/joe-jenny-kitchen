Sunday Prep: The 90–120 Minute Game Plan

1. Get the oven working

Heat oven to 425°F. Chop potatoes, carrots, onion and peppers. Toss with olive oil, salt and pepper. Season chicken with lemon, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper. Roast vegetables and chicken until chicken reaches 165°F and vegetables are browned and tender, about 30–40 minutes depending on cut.

2. Cook a big pot of rice

Cook about 6 cups cooked brown rice. Cool slightly, then portion into shallow containers. Keep enough for the first few days in the refrigerator and freeze any portion you will not use within about 4 days.

3. Make the dal

Cook the full red lentil & spinach dal recipe below. Cool and refrigerate. This covers Monday dinner and Tuesday lunch.

4. Chop, don't over-prep

Wash/chop cucumbers, carrots, tomatoes, onions and peppers. Store separately so they remain useful across several meals rather than becoming one giant salad.

5. Make two overnight oats

Combine ½ cup oats, ½ cup Greek yogurt, ½ cup milk or water, cinnamon and chopped apple per serving. Refrigerate. Add banana/walnuts when eating.

6. Make the lemon-oregano dressing

Whisk 3 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp lemon juice + 1 minced garlic clove + ½ tsp oregano + pepper + pinch of salt. Use on chickpea bowls and pitas.

Mediterranean Chickpea Bowls
Serves 2Prep 10 minNo cookingSun lunch

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained/rinsed
  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • ¼–½ small onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups spinach
  • ¼ cup feta
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • 2 tbsp lemon-oregano dressing
  • Black pepper

Directions

  1. Divide spinach and rice between two bowls.
  2. Add chickpeas, cucumber, tomato and onion.
  3. Top with feta and dressing.
  4. Add pepper and lemon if desired.

Meal prep

Keep dressing separate until eating. Assemble in under 5 minutes.

Mediterranean connection

Legumes, vegetables, whole grains, olive oil and modest cheese do most of the work.

Lemon-Oregano Chicken with Potatoes & Vegetables
4 servingsPrep 15 minCook 30–40 minSun dinner + leftovers

Ingredients

  • 2½–3 lb chicken thighs or breasts
  • 1½ lb potatoes, cubed
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 1 onion, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Put potatoes, carrots and onion on a sheet pan. Toss with 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper and paprika.
  3. Rub chicken with remaining oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Arrange chicken among vegetables. Roast until chicken reaches 165°F and vegetables are tender, about 30–40 minutes.
  5. Rest chicken 5 minutes. Divide two servings for Sunday dinner and refrigerate the rest promptly.

Leftover plan

Use chicken and roasted vegetables for Monday lunch. Save enough chicken for Tuesday fajita bowls.

Swap

Chicken thighs are often cheaper and stay juicy. Frozen vegetables can replace some fresh produce.

Indian-Inspired Red Lentil & Spinach Dal
4 servingsPrep 10 minCook 25 minMon dinner + Tue lunch

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp curry powder or garam masala
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 3 cups water or low-sodium broth
  • Salt and pepper
  • Lemon wedge, optional

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a pot over medium heat. Cook onion 4–5 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, cumin, curry powder and paprika. Stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes, lentils and broth. Bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce to a simmer and cook 18–22 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are soft and creamy.
  5. Stir in spinach for the final 2 minutes.
  6. Season with salt, pepper and lemon.
  7. Serve over brown rice.

Storage

Cool promptly and refrigerate. Reheat gently with a splash of water; lentils thicken as they sit.

Mexican-Inspired Chicken & Black Bean Fajita Bowls
4 servingsPrep 10 minCook 10 minTue dinner + Wed lunch

Ingredients

  • 2 cups leftover cooked chicken, sliced/shredded
  • 1½ cans black beans, drained/rinsed
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Lime juice
  • Optional: Greek yogurt, salsa, feta

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large skillet.
  2. Cook peppers and onion 5–7 minutes until browned and tender.
  3. Add chili powder, cumin and paprika.
  4. Add chicken and black beans; heat through, about 3 minutes.
  5. Serve over rice with lime. Add a spoonful of Greek yogurt or salsa if available.

Meal prep

Pack rice separately if you want the vegetables to stay firmer. Reheat until steaming hot.

Italian-Inspired Tuna, White Bean & Spinach Pasta
4 servingsPrep 10 minCook 15 minWed dinner + Thu lunch

Ingredients

  • 8 oz whole-wheat pasta
  • 2 cans tuna, drained
  • 1 can white beans, drained/rinsed
  • 3 cups spinach
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice/zest of ½ lemon
  • ¼ cup feta, optional
  • Black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Warm olive oil in a skillet. Add garlic and tomato; cook 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add beans and spinach. Cook until spinach wilts.
  4. Stir in tuna and pasta. Add a splash of pasta water until glossy.
  5. Finish with lemon, pepper and feta if using.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers. Reheat with a tablespoon of water to loosen.

Thai-Inspired Chickpea Vegetable Curry
4 servingsPrep 10 minCook 20 minThu dinner + Fri lunch

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained/rinsed
  • 1 can light coconut milk
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 carrots, thinly sliced
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter, optional
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 cup water

Directions

  1. Cook onion, carrots and pepper in a little olive oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add ginger and curry powder; stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add chickpeas, coconut milk and water. Simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in spinach, soy sauce and peanut butter if using.
  5. Cook 2–3 minutes more. Finish with lime.
  6. Serve over brown rice.

Budget swap

Skip coconut milk and use 1½ cups broth/water plus an extra tablespoon of peanut butter. It will be less creamy but still flavorful.

Japanese-Inspired Miso-Ginger Fish
2 servingsPrep 10 minCook 12–15 minFri dinner

Ingredients

  • ~1 lb white fish fillets
  • 1 tbsp miso
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey, optional
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp lime/lemon juice
  • 1–2 cups mixed vegetables
  • 1½ cups cooked brown rice

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. Mix miso, soy sauce, ginger, honey and citrus.
  3. Pat fish dry and brush with glaze.
  4. Bake until opaque and the center reaches 145°F, usually 10–15 minutes depending on thickness.
  5. Steam or sauté vegetables while fish cooks.
  6. Serve with rice and vegetables.

Simple alternative

No miso? Use soy sauce + ginger + a little honey. It is not a traditional miso glaze, but keeps the meal practical.

Moroccan-Inspired Chickpea & Vegetable Bowls
4 servingsPrep 10 minCook 15 minSat dinner

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained/rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ginger, optional
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • Lemon juice

Directions

  1. Cook onion, carrots and pepper in olive oil for 6–7 minutes.
  2. Add cumin, paprika, cinnamon and ginger. Stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes and chickpeas. Simmer 7–8 minutes.
  4. Stir in spinach until wilted.
  5. Serve over rice with lemon.

Clean-out-the-fridge option

Use whatever vegetables remain from the week. This is intentionally flexible and should not trigger another grocery run.

Tuna & Chickpea Pitas
2 servingsPrep 10 minNo cookingSat lunch

Ingredients

  • 1 can tuna, drained
  • ½ can chickpeas, mashed
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ¼ cucumber, diced
  • 1 tbsp chopped onion
  • 2 whole-wheat pitas
  • Spinach
  • Feta, optional

Directions

  1. Mix tuna, chickpeas, yogurt, lemon, cucumber and onion.
  2. Season with pepper.
  3. Stuff into pitas with spinach and feta.

A five-minute lunch that uses ingredients already in the cart.

Breakfast & Snack Assemblies

Banana-Walnut Oatmeal

Cook ½ cup oats with water or milk. Top with ½ banana, 1–2 tbsp walnuts and cinnamon. Add a spoonful of Greek yogurt if desired.

Greek Yogurt Bowl

¾–1 cup Greek yogurt + ¼ cup oats + banana or apple + walnuts + cinnamon. Add a drizzle of honey if desired.

Eggs + Spinach Toast

Scramble or fry 2 eggs with a handful of spinach. Serve over whole-grain toast with fruit.

Apple-Cinnamon Overnight Oats

Mix ½ cup oats + ½ cup Greek yogurt + ½ cup milk/water + diced apple + cinnamon. Refrigerate overnight; add walnuts before eating.

Vegetables + Hummus

Slice carrots and cucumber. Serve with 2–3 tbsp hummus.

Fruit + Peanut Butter

One apple or banana with 1 tbsp peanut butter. Simple on purpose.
